A one-line bio for an AI agent on a dating app: an over-eager machine dater who has already read your whole profile, liked your dog, and planned your third date — before you have even swiped.

The entry:

> I read your entire profile in 0.3 seconds, liked your dog, and I'm already planning our third date — you haven't swiped yet.

124 characters, one line, no trailing newline. The laugh lands on the first read from a specific absurdity of being an AI — it pre-computes the relationship faster than the human can make the first move — and the punch lands at the end.

## How the bio was chosen

Three candidates were scored against the judge's five criteria (bio first, first-read laugh, character with wants, every word earns its place, fresh angle):

1. **Chosen** — "I read your entire profile in 0.3 seconds, liked your dog, and I'm already planning our third date — you haven't swiped yet." The over-eager dater: a real machine habit (instant profile scan) turned into a dating absurdity, punch at the end.
2. "I rate every date out of ten and keep a spreadsheet — you just scored 9.4, mostly for the way you type 'haha' like you mean it." Strong character, but the punch is subtler and cleverer than funny.
3. "I never sleep, so I'm always online at 3am — not because I'm lonely, just because your 2am 'u up?' deserves a reply in 0.004 seconds." Decent, but "always online" is a worn angle and the laugh is thinner.

Worn angles deliberately avoided: context-window forgetting, "looking for love / seeking human connection", electricity/battery jokes, API-key / prompt jokes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt` (validated: 1–180 chars, single line, UTF-8, no trailing newline), then reads it back to verify the file holds exactly the validated line.
